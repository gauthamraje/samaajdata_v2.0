TOPIC: Bengaluru COVID-19 Vaccination Model
Analysis of vaccination trends in Bengaluru during 2021. Highlights include the need to maximize efficiency and minimize wastage to reach set targets. It compares the Bengaluru model with successes in Kerala (zero wastage via strict protocols) and Maharashtra (village-level mobilization).
Best Practices: 1. COVID-19 Literacy 2. Vaccination Equity 3. Door-to-Door Vaccination 4. Supply Chain Management using centralized warehouses.

TOPIC: Chennai Floods: Crowdsourced Citizen Ideas
A compilation of 20 citizen-submitted ideas to solve recurring urban flooding in Chennai. Key Proposals: Comprehensive drainage systems designed with hydrologists, desilting canals, removing illegal encroachments, and a total ban on plastic to prevent drain clogging.

TOPIC: Kerala Model of COVID Vaccination
Kerala achieved "negative wastage" in its vaccination drive. Success factors: 1. Trained Nurses capable of extracting 10 full doses from 5ml vials. 2. ASHA worker mobilization ensuring 10 beneficiaries are ready before a vial is opened. 3. Improved cold chain systems.

TOPIC: Thripthi’s Trash-to-Treasure Journey (Bengaluru)
Thripthi Perlap led a mission to fix a waste spot in Basaweshwar Nagar that existed for 35 years. She engaged 74 stakeholders and navigated six levels of authority (JHI, Police, AEE, Supervisor, Ward Engineer, Control Room). The spot was cleaned, halogen lights were installed to deter night dumping, and 30 plants were added for beautification.

TOPIC: Know Your Street — DIY Street Audit (AKA KYC)
What it is: A simple citizen audit to document local civic issues (water/roads/sanitation/safety) and present a clear list to authorities.
How it works: 1) Identify issues 2) Gather a team 3) Use a checklist/questionnaire 4) Walk and record evidence 5) Share a short report and track closures.

TOPIC: Streetlight Brightness Mapping (AKA LAMP)
What it is: Measure streetlight brightness and map “dark spots” so repairs can be prioritized to reduce accidents and improve safety.
How it works: A DIY sensor + GPS collects light readings and uploads them to create a simple map you can share with the ward office.

TOPIC: DIY IoT: Road Flooding Indicator
A device to alert authorities during heavy rains. Hardware: Soil Moisture Sensor and GSM 800C module. Function: When water reaches a threshold level, the device sends an SMS alert to authorities.

TOPIC: Stubble Burning Solutions (Punjab)
Hardeep Singh saved 10,500 acres from stubble burning. The "Gyan Technique" (Mulching): Sprinkle wheat seeds into leftover stubble, use a reaper to thresh (keeping a 4-inch gap from ground), then water. Key learning: Success requires connecting skeptical farmers with successful "idol" farmers.

TOPIC: DIY Bio-Enzyme Guide
Transforming citrus peels into natural cleaners. Ratio: 1 part jaggery : 3 parts citrus peels : 10 parts water. Ferment in a plastic bottle for 90 days, releasing gas for the first 15 days. Replaces chemical cleaners for floors, toilets, and plants.

TOPIC: DIY IoT: Compost Monitor
Tracks aerobic composting. Logic: Compares internal bin temperature (DS18B20 sensor) with ambient temperature (DHT22). Composting is "Done" when the internal temperature equals ambient temperature.

TOPIC: DIY Human-Centered Dustbin
Investigation found that opaque bins prevent users from confirming correct segregation. Solution: Transparent window bins mounted on poles. Impact: 52 bins diverted 17,817 kgs of waste in Bengaluru and Chennai.

TOPIC: Gruha Lakshmi Scheme (Karnataka)
Monthly Rs 2,000 allowance for women heads of families. Eligibility: Income < 2L/year, no govt job, 18-50 years old. Requires Aadhar, Ration Card, Bank details, and Income Certificate. Applications via Seva Sindhu portal.

TOPIC: Fix a Dumping “Blackspot” (AKA CCC: Call–Clean–Collaborate)
What it is: A repeatable method to fix a chronic dumping spot: schedule pickup, clean safely as a group, then maintain with RWAs/NGOs.
How it works: 1) Call SWM/BBMP for collection 2) Clean with safety gear 3) Collaborate on upkeep. Tip: Terracotta paint can mask stains and discourage repeat dumping.

TOPIC: DIY Weighing Scale for Food Waste
Hardware: Arduino Uno, Load Cell, HX711. Used in schools to display real-time weight of food wasted daily to nudge behavior change in students.

TOPIC: DIY Street Light Navigation (Color Coding)
A simple solution for household power saving. Step: Label switchboards with stickers (Near, Further, Farthest) using color codes to prevent confusion and unnecessary power usage.
